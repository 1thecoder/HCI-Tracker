This is a normal-looking page with three deliberate usability problems planted in it. See if the tracker catches them.

  1. Move your mouse around and click a few things.
  2. Click the grey Add to cart boxes several times quickly — they are fake buttons, so you should trigger dead clicks and rage clicks.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and fill in the order form.
  4. Open the purple Tracking pill at the bottom right, or press Ctrl Shift U.
  5. Check the Overview score, then switch on the Heatmap.
  6. Open the full analytics dashboard for charts, funnels and the complete event log.

Our story

We started as a single cart outside the engineering block, pulling shots on a machine that needed a firm hand and a lot of patience. Today we roast our own beans two streets away and serve around four hundred students a day, most of them in the fifteen minutes between lectures.

This block of text exists so the page is tall enough to scroll properly. The tracker measures how far down the page you actually get, which is one of the clearest signals of whether content below the fold is ever seen at all.

In a real usability study you would compare that number across several participants. If most people stop at forty percent, anything placed below that point is effectively invisible, no matter how important you think it is.

“Where the pointer rests is a rough proxy for where attention goes — so the cold areas on a heatmap are the ones worth questioning.”
